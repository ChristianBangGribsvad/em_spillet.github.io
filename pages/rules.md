# Rules & Point System

## Match Predictions

For every group stage match you predict the exact final score.
Points depend on how close your prediction is:

<div class="rules-table">
<div class="rules-row rules-header">
  <span>Points</span><span>What you got right</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-15">
  <span class="rules-pts">15</span>
  <span>Exact score — both teams correct</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-10">
  <span class="rules-pts">10</span>
  <span>Correct outcome <em>and</em> one team's exact score</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-5">
  <span class="rules-pts">5</span>
  <span>Correct outcome only (right winner or draw)</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-2">
  <span class="rules-pts">2</span>
  <span>One team's exact score — but wrong outcome</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-0">
  <span class="rules-pts">0</span>
  <span>Nothing correct</span>
</div>
</div>

**Example — actual result: Mexico 2–1 South Africa**

| Your prediction | Points | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **2–1** | 15 | Exact score |
| **3–1** | 10 | Mexico win ✓ + South Africa score 1 ✓ |
| **2–0** | 10 | Mexico win ✓ + Mexico score 2 ✓ |
| **1–0** | 5 | Mexico win ✓, no score match |
| **0–1** | 2 | South Africa score 1 ✓, but wrong outcome |
| **1–1** | 2 | Wrong outcome, but South Africa score (1) is correct |
| **0–0** | 0 | Wrong outcome, no score correct |

## How the Game Works

The site updates automatically every 20 minutes. Scores, charts and standings are refreshed within minutes of a match finishing.

<div class="rules-table">
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Schedule</strong></span>
  <span>Runs at :03, :23 and :43 past each hour</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Score updates</strong></span>
  <span>When new results are detected, everyone's totals are recalculated and all pages regenerated</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Schedule sections</strong></span>
  <span><em>Next Matches</em> and <em>Yesterday's Results</em> refresh every 20 min even when no scores change</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Today's Schmeichel</strong></span>
  <span>Whoever earned the most points since the previous scoring update</span>
</div>
</div>

## How to Force-Refresh the Page

Browsers and phones cache pages for speed. After scores update you may need to force a reload to bypass the cache:

<div class="rules-table">
<div class="rules-row rules-header">
  <span>Device</span><span>How to refresh</span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Windows</strong><br><small>Chrome / Firefox / Edge</small></span>
  <span><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd></span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Mac</strong><br><small>Chrome / Firefox</small></span>
  <span><kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd></span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>Mac</strong><br><small>Safari</small></span>
  <span><kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Option</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd></span>
</div>
<div class="rules-row pts-unplayed">
  <span><strong>iPhone / iPad</strong><br><small>Safari</small></span>
  <span>Open a new <strong>Private tab</strong> (tap the tabs icon → Private) and navigate to the page. This is the simplest method. Alternatively: <em>Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data</em>.</span>
</div>
</div>
